City and state comptroller audits of New York City government

The audit files

New York City government gets audited constantly, by both the city comptroller and the state comptroller, but the results land in long PDFs that few people read. This tracker collects every audit of city agencies and programs since January 1, 2026 and restates what the auditors examined, what they found and what they told the agency to fix, in plain language, with links to every official source.

About this tracker. Audits of New York City agencies, programs and spending issued on or after January 1, 2026 by the New York City Comptroller's Office and the Office of the New York State Comptroller. Plain-language summaries are drawn only from the text of each official audit page; every card links to the official report and PDF. Full-length reports, appendices and agency response letters live at the source links.

Methodology
This tracker was built with AI assistance. The audit list, dates, agencies and source links come directly from the two comptrollers' official websites, and every summary is written from the text of the official audit pages, but AI-written summaries can contain errors of emphasis or interpretation. Before relying on any figure or finding, read the official report linked on each card.

Built by Josh Greenman. Data from comptroller.nyc.gov and osc.ny.gov.
