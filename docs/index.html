Comptroller audits, investigations, court monitor reports and oversight board reports on New York City government

The audit files

New York City government answers to a small army of official watchdogs: the city comptroller, the state comptroller, the Department of Investigation, court-appointed monitors and independent oversight boards. Their findings land in long PDFs that few people read. This tracker collects every report on city agencies and programs since January 1, 2025 and restates what the watchdogs examined, what they found, what they told the agency to fix and what has actually been fixed, in plain language, with links to every official source.

About this tracker. Audits, investigations and monitor reports on New York City agencies, programs and spending issued on or after January 1, 2025 by the New York City Comptroller's Office, the Office of the New York State Comptroller, the New York City Department of Investigation, court-appointed monitors in the Floyd stop and frisk, Nunez jails, NYCHA public housing and L.V. special education cases, the New York City Board of Correction and the Commission to Combat Police Corruption. Plain-language summaries are drawn only from the text of each official report; every card links to the official source. Implementation statuses for city comptroller audits come from the comptroller's own Audit Recommendations Tracker and are agency-reported.

Methodology
This tracker was built with AI assistance. Report lists, dates, agencies, source links and implementation statuses come directly from official government websites, and every summary is written from the text of the official reports, but AI-written summaries can contain errors of emphasis or interpretation. Before relying on any figure or finding, read the official report linked on each card.
